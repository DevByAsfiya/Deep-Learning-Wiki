## How to take a course properly

Watching lectures produces recognition, not recall. Three rules that change the
outcome:

1. **Do every assignment**, without looking at the solution first. The gap
   between watching and doing is where the learning is.
2. **Pause and predict.** Before the lecturer reveals an answer, write down your
   own. Being wrong is more instructive than being told.
3. **Build something unrelated** halfway through. Applying a technique to a
   problem the course never mentioned is the real test of transfer.
